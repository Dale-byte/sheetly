Sheetly — Full Local Backup

This page only reads your data. It never writes, migrates, or deletes anything. Open it on the device whose data you want to copy.

Why this exists. The app's own Export and Backup buttons leave out debts and payslips, and Import silently drops tags, debts and payslips entirely. Those buttons are not a safe backup. This one reads the full local state instead, and also flags the two cases where the app deletes data on its own.

Do step 2 at minimum. Step 3 is a byte-exact copy of the stored key and is the most faithful restore source. Keep all files until you have confirmed your data is intact.
